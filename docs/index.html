Bybit Futures Scanner

Runs directly in your iPhone browser using Bybit public market data. No login, API keys, or trades. Scans automatically while this browser page is open; it does not run when the page is closed.

Tap Scan now to check connectivity.

Only closed 5-minute candles; liquidity threshold $1m 24h; checks all eligible USDT perpetual contracts (may take several minutes). READY TO REVIEW means screening criteria pass, not that a trade should be opened. WAIT means more confirmation or a better quote is needed. Only READY TO REVIEW setups are displayed; WAIT and AVOID are hidden. No signal is a guarantee. TOP 10 excludes extended and negative-score setups, and shows only confirmed momentum with an acceptable preliminary entry screen, a refreshed Bybit bid/ask quote, and live R:R of at least 1:1.5. Entry quote uses ask for LONG and bid for SHORT; stop and target remain candle-derived, so live R:R is not automatically 1:2. TOP 10 shows up to ten eligible signals, with LONG first, SHORT second, and score descending within each direction. If the quote is missing, spread exceeds 0.2%, or the quote has moved more than the smaller of 0.5% and half the initial stop distance, the entry is marked WAIT. Quotes refresh automatically every 10 seconds while this page is open and visible; the full indicator scan refreshes approximately every five minutes while the page remains open. A failed refresh marks quotes stale. Only the ten highest ranked eligible signals are displayed. Unconfirmed signals are always marked WAIT. Ranking combines confirmation, stage, score and stop distance. *Entry/stop/target are indicative levels from the last closed 5m candle, recent swing and 14-candle average true range, NOT live executable quotes. Target uses 2 times initial stop distance (1:2 before fees, slippage and funding). Entry check is a screening flag, not a trading instruction. Recheck current bid/ask, spread, contract tick size and order book before placing any order. Stop orders can slip and liquidation may occur before a stop at high leverage. Confirmation requires aligned 5m/15m proxy trends, RSI, volume and 15m price momentum; it is not a guarantee. Early detection uses recent volume buildup with limited price movement; this is not predictive. Trend compares the last closed 5m price against 30m and 45m ago (a proxy, not independent 15m candle confirmation). Liquidity is 24h turnover in USD. EARLY / DEVELOPING / EXTENDED are heuristic labels, not predictions. Score is a relative ranking, not a win probability. No signal is a trade recommendation. Market prices may move before you act. If Bybit blocks browser requests or CORS, the scanner shows an error rather than invented results.
