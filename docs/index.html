Bybit Futures Scanner

Runs directly in your iPhone browser using Bybit public market data. No login, API keys, or trades. This is an on-demand scanner, not background monitoring.

Tap Scan now to check connectivity.

Only closed 5-minute candles; liquidity threshold $1m 24h; checks all eligible USDT perpetual contracts (may take several minutes). TOP 3 shows only confirmed momentum with an acceptable preliminary entry screen AND a refreshed Bybit bid/ask quote. LONG candidates appear before SHORT candidates within TOP 3. If the quote is missing, spread exceeds 0.2%, or the quote has moved more than the smaller of 0.5% and half the initial stop distance, the entry is marked WAIT. Quotes are snapshots after scanning, not streaming. Remaining LONG signals appear before SHORT signals. Unconfirmed signals are always marked WAIT. Ranking combines confirmation, stage, score and stop distance. *Entry/stop/target are indicative levels from the last closed 5m candle, recent swing and 14-candle average true range, NOT live executable quotes. Target uses 2 times initial stop distance (1:2 before fees, slippage and funding). Entry check is a screening flag, not a trading instruction. Recheck current bid/ask, spread, contract tick size and order book before placing any order. Stop orders can slip and liquidation may occur before a stop at high leverage. Confirmation requires aligned 5m/15m proxy trends, RSI, volume and 15m price momentum; it is not a guarantee. Early detection uses recent volume buildup with limited price movement; this is not predictive. Trend compares the last closed 5m price against 30m and 45m ago (a proxy, not independent 15m candle confirmation). Liquidity is 24h turnover in USD. EARLY / DEVELOPING / EXTENDED are heuristic labels, not predictions. Score is a relative ranking, not a win probability. No signal is a trade recommendation. Market prices may move before you act. If Bybit blocks browser requests or CORS, the scanner shows an error rather than invented results.
